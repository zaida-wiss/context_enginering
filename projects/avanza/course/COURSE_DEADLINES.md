## CTO Feed Forward — Graded Deliverable

**Submission Deadline:** Thursday, September 24, 2026 @ 4:00 PM  
**Feedback Delivery:** Monday, September 28, 2026 @ 4:00 PM

### What's Required

Team (without PL) records a **max 5-minute video** showing how risk analysis has **actually shaped project decisions** — not just what risks exist.

**Video must demonstrate:**

- **Trade-offs** — What technical choices did we make *because of* identified risks? (Eliminated? Simplified? Changed?)
- **Mitigations** — How realistic and verifiable are risk controls? Effort vs. benefit proportional?
- **Impact on Solution** — Did risk analysis lead to different technical approach/architecture than originally planned? Provide concrete example.
- **Uncertainties** — What still needs investigation/testing before we can make solid decisions?

### Submission Process

1. Record a screen-share video (use Loom, Zoom, or similar)
2. Create a shareable link
3. Open: [Chas Extended Challenge – Specialist Feed Forward](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/[TO_BE_FILLED])
4. Find your team row
5. Paste link under **"CTO – teamfilm"**
6. Verify link opens without permission request

**Important:** Do not send separately — all submissions go in the shared Google Sheet only.

### After Feedback (Sept 28+)

**Victor may intentionally give more feedback than you can realistically complete.**  
**Part of this assignment is learning to interpret, estimate effort, prioritize, and decide what's important vs. good enough.**

Your job (Team + PL together):

- Decide what you can actually deliver before end of course

**Goal:** Make smart decisions about priority, not do everything.
