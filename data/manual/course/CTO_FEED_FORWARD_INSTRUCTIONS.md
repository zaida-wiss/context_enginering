## What the Video Must Show

Video should demonstrate how risk work has **shaped actual decisions**. Choose concrete examples:

### Trade-offs
- What choices did we make *because* of identified risks?
- Did we eliminate, simplify, or change something to reduce risk?

### Mitigations
- How realistic, achievable, and verifiable are our risk controls?
- How do we know they work? Is the effort proportional to the risk reduction?

### Impact on Solution
- Did risk analysis lead to different technical choices, architecture, or approach than initially planned?
- Provide a concrete example.

### Uncertainties
- What areas still need investigation, testing, or verification before we can make solid decisions?

**Tip:** Start with your updated risk analysis on screen, but select examples that best show *actual impact*.
